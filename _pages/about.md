I'm Marko. I live in Taipei and spend most of my days in Go services and cloud systems, figuring out how software should behave when things go wrong, not only when demos go right.

I care about clear trade-offs, code that other people can understand, and products that actually help someone finish a job. That's the thread through my open-source work and the products I'm building at [DuReef](https://dureef.com/), including [Cloud Coach Atlas](https://cloud-coach.dureef.com/).
