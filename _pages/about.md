I'm Marko. I live in Taipei and spend most of my days in Go services and cloud systems, figuring out how software should behave when things go wrong, not only when demos go right.

I care about clear trade-offs, code that other people can understand, and products that actually help someone finish a job. That's the thread through my open-source work and the products I'm building at [DuReef](https://dureef.com/), including [DuReef Learn](https://cloud-coach.dureef.com/).

## What I do best
- **Go backend**: services, APIs, performance, concurrency
- **Cloud engineering**: AWS, infrastructure, security, observability, cost
- **Technical leadership**: reviews, decisions, mentoring
- **Developer enablement**: workshops, documentation, and learning resources

## Selected work
- **DuReef Learn**: a learning platform from [DuReef](https://dureef.com/) for cloud and other technical topics, built around focused paths, practice, guidance, and clear progress ([open DuReef Learn](https://cloud-coach.dureef.com/))
- **Open-source projects**: Go, backend, cloud, automation, and developer tooling on [GitHub](https://github.com/marko-durasic)
- **Technical writing**: practical notes and tutorials on backend and cloud engineering

## What I'm exploring
- **System design** as a practical craft: trade-offs, failure modes, and operational reality
- **Systems thinking**: constraints, feedback loops, and making complexity legible
- **Using AI smartly**: faster iteration with strong tests, review habits, and clear reasoning
