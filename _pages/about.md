I’m Marko, a software engineer originally from Belgrade and based in Taipei since 2010.
I work mainly on backend and cloud systems, most often with Go and AWS.

I like software that is easy to reason about, behaves predictably in production, and does not hide
avoidable complexity behind fashionable abstractions. I also like learning by building small tools,
writing down what I discover, and revisiting assumptions when the evidence changes.

## Software

My work and interests tend to circle around:

- backend services, APIs, performance, and concurrency;
- AWS architecture, infrastructure, reliability, security, and cost;
- system design and the operational trade-offs behind it;
- developer tools and practical uses of AI in engineering;
- Linux and open-source software.
